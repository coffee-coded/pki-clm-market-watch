## Topics / keywords

- Certificate Lifecycle Management (CLM) / Certificate Automation
- PKI-as-a-Service
- Machine identity management / non-human identity management
- Post-quantum cryptography migration (NIST PQC standards, crypto-agility)
- Code signing management
- IoT device identity / IoT PKI
- Short-lived certificates / 47-day (and shrinking) TLS certificate lifespans
  (CA/Browser Forum ballots)
- Kubernetes / cloud-native certificate automation (cert-manager, SPIFFE/SPIRE)
- Certificate-related outages or incidents (expired-cert outages are a
  recurring, newsworthy failure mode in this space)
- M&A, funding rounds, leadership changes among the above vendors
- Analyst coverage (Gartner, Forrester) naming these vendors

## Explicitly out of scope

- General enterprise IAM/SSO (Okta, Ping, etc.) unless a story specifically
  intersects with PKI/CLM/machine identity
- General cybersecurity news unrelated to certificates/PKI
