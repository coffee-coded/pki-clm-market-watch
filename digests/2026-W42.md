## Significant

### AppViewX ships an Agent Kill Switch and Shadow AI discovery
On October 6, AppViewX shipped a major expansion of Agent Identity Security
(built on its Eos acquisition): an "Agent Kill Switch" that can stop both
sanctioned and shadow AI agents automatically, via policy, or manually; new
shadow-AI/MCP-server discovery; agent identities chained to AppViewX-managed
PKI; and AI cost controls. This is a primary-vendor major capability launch
— lands three weeks after DigiCert's own AI Trust Manager kill switch (GA
Sept 15), confirming AI-agent identity is now a genuine three-way
competitive front between AppViewX, DigiCert, and Keyfactor (via its
pending Cofide acquisition).

## Correction

### Venafi/Certificate Manager is NOT rebranding to "Idira" (at least for now)
The 2026-09-25 finding overstated things, saying flatly that "Venafi is now
sold as Idira Certificate Manager." Re-checked against CyberArk's own FAQ
this week: the Certificates/PKI portfolio is explicitly the **exception**
to the Idira rename and stays CyberArk-branded in this rollout phase, even
though several third-party sites call it "Idira Certificate Manager"
(apparently premature). **This tracker now treats "CyberArk Certificate
Manager" as the authoritative current name** for this product going
forward, while the broader Idira corporate rebrand itself remains accurate.

after their actual publication date across the life of this tracker (Idira
rebrand, DigiCert breach, DigiCert AI Trust Manager, Sectigo Quantum Ready,
DigiCert Quantum Central) — plus one correction needed on a claim this

same-day secondary-vendor and standalone-security stories consistently.
Worth continuing the periodic wider sweeps, and double-checking any
confident factual claim about corporate branding/ownership against the
primary source (not third-party blogs) before reporting it as settled.
