# Baseline retrospective — September 2024 to September 2026

One-time backfill covering the two years before daily tracking started
(2026-09-15). Scope: only news that shifted how a vendor or the market
moves — ownership changes, M&A, funding, leadership, and structural/
regulatory shifts that force buyers and vendors to act. Routine product
updates and minor news are deliberately excluded here; see `findings/` for
day-to-day detail going forward.

## Timeline

### 2024-05 — CyberArk signs definitive agreement to acquire Venafi ($1.54B)
CyberArk agreed to buy Venafi from Thoma Bravo for ~$1.54B cash+stock.
First step in Venafi ceasing to be an independent company.
[CyberArk press release](https://www.cyberark.com/press/cyberark-signs-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-machine-identity-management-leader-venafi-from-thoma-bravo/) · [SDxCentral](https://www.sdxcentral.com/news/cyberark-to-acquire-venafi-for-154b-to-secure-human-and-machine-identities/)

### 2024 (announced Apr, closed later in 2024) — IBM acquires HashiCorp ($6.4B)
IBM bought HashiCorp (Vault, Terraform) for $35/share (~$6.4B enterprise
value). HashiCorp Vault's PKI secrets engine — a secondary competitor on
this watchlist — is now part of IBM/Red Hat's automation portfolio rather
than an independent product roadmap.
[Medium: On IBM acquiring HashiCorp](https://medium.com/@fintanr/on-ibm-acquiring-hashicorp-c9c73a40d20c) · [GovConWire](https://www.govconwire.com/articles/ibm-closes-hashicorp-acquisition)

### 2024-10-01 — CyberArk completes acquisition of Venafi
Deal closed; Venafi contributed ~$41M revenue and $11M operating income to
CyberArk in Q4 2024 alone, adding $164M ARR. CyberArk's total addressable
market grew ~$10B (to ~$60B) on the back of this deal. **Venafi is now a
CyberArk business unit, not an independent vendor** — material for how you
read any "Venafi" news from this point forward. (Its product has since been
rebranded "CyberArk Certificate Manager.")
[CyberArk: Completes Acquisition](https://www.cyberark.com/press/cyberark-completes-acquisition-of-venafi/) · [DarkReading](https://www.darkreading.com/cloud-security/cyberark-picks-up-machine-id-manager-venafi-for-1-54b)

### 2024-11-21 (completed 2025-01-06) — Haveli Investments acquires controlling interest in AppViewX
Haveli Investments (Austin-based PE) took a controlling/majority stake in
AppViewX. **AppViewX ownership changed hands** — no longer founder/VC-led
in the same way; Haveli's capital and "operating expertise" now drive
product/go-to-market decisions.
[Businesswire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241121535189/en/Haveli-Investments-Acquires-Controlling-Interest-in-Machine-Identity-Leader-AppViewX) · [AppViewX news](https://www.appviewx.com/news/haveli-investments-completes-acquisition-of-appviewx/)

### 2025-01 — Keyfactor appoints Michael Volanoski as President & CRO
Leadership build-out under CEO Jordan Rackie (who succeeded co-founder
Kevin von Keyserling; von Keyserling moved to Chief Strategy Officer).
Signals a scale-up phase for go-to-market ahead of the much larger 2026
funding round below.
[Keyfactor press release](https://www.keyfactor.com/press-releases/keyfactor-appoints-michael-volanoski-as-president-chief-revenue-officer-to-extend-market-leadership-in-the-ai-and-quantum-era/)

### 2025-01-29 (migration completed 2025-09-18) — Entrust sells its entire public certificate business to Sectigo
Entrust exited the public CA market outright after CA/Browser Forum
compliance violations led Google/Mozilla to distrust its certificates.
Sectigo absorbed the business, **doubling its enterprise footprint** — one
of the biggest consolidation moves in the CA/CLM market this period.
Entrust itself pivoted to focus on quantum-ready cryptographic data
security instead of public certs.
[Entrust newsroom](https://www.entrust.com/company/newsroom/entrust-sells-public-certificate-business-to-sectigo) · [Sectigo](https://www.sectigo.com/resource-library/sectigo-acquires-entrust-public-certificate-business) · [BiometricUpdate](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/entrust-sharpens-cryptographic-data-security-focus-with-public-certificate-divestment)

### 2025-04-11 — CA/Browser Forum passes Ballot SC-081v3: certificate lifespans cut to 47 days
Apple-proposed, Sectigo-endorsed ballot passed 29–0. Phased rollout:
**200 days from March 2026, 100 days from March 2027, 47 days from March
2029.** This is the single biggest structural driver in the space for this
whole period — it makes manual certificate management operationally
impossible at scale and is the direct tailwind behind every CLM vendor's
"automation or outages" pitch (see the Microsoft 365 outage entry in
`findings/2026-09-15.md` for a live example of the failure mode this ballot
targets).
[DigiCert blog](https://www.digicert.com/blog/tls-certificate-lifetimes-will-officially-reduce-to-47-days) · [Slashdot](https://it.slashdot.org/story/25/04/19/1745216/cabrowser-forum-votes-for-47-day-cert-durations-by-2029) · [Sectigo](https://www.sectigo.com/resource-library/sectigo-cab-reduce-ssl-tls-certificates-lifespan-47-days)

## What this means for reading the space going forward

- **Venafi is two acquisitions deep** (CyberArk, then Palo Alto Networks) —
  it no longer moves on its own strategy; watch Palo Alto's platform
  announcements, not just Venafi's.
- **AppViewX is PE-owned** (Haveli) and has started making its own
  acquisitions (Eos) — expect more bolt-on M&A funded by Haveli.
- **Keyfactor is the one still expanding as an independent, VC/PE-backed
  company** — biggest war chest ($1B+), most aggressive M&A cadence
  (Cofide), and the only one of the three explicitly building for AI-agent
  identity.
- **The 47-day certificate ballot (2025-04) is the structural constant**
  behind almost every vendor pitch and buyer urgency in this space through
  2029 — treat any vendor news through that lens.
- **The CA/public-cert layer consolidated hard**: Entrust is out, Sectigo
  absorbed Entrust's book and rebranded around scale, DigiCert kept
  acquiring adjacent products (Valimail). Fewer, larger public CAs.
