## Significant

### DigiCert discloses breach of archived customer data, possibly including identity documents
An unauthorized account read a subset of DigiCert's archived customer
records (names, emails, phone numbers, business addresses) during a
June 27–July 2, 2026 access window; DigiCert confirmed the scope
internally Oct 19 and disclosed publicly in early November. The Register
reports exposed data may also include identity documents for a "small
minority" of accounts — a more severe characterization than DigiCert's
own framing. CA issuance/validation systems were not affected; this is a
business-systems breach, distinct from September's EV code-signing
breach. A disclosed breach involving a watchlist company meets this
tracker's SIGNIFICANT bar.

## Routine

### Cloudflare announces plans to become a public Certificate Authority
Backfill catch: announced Sept 29, 2026, missed for about seven weeks.
Cloudflare plans to issue both classical TLS and post-quantum Merkle Tree
Certificates via ACME, backed initially by a GlobalSign root with
applications pending in four browser root programs; post-quantum issuance
targeted for early 2027. A major new entrant to the public-CA market
(previously sourced certs from 16 partner CAs) — doesn't cleanly meet this
tracker's SIGNIFICANT criteria (no watchlist vendor, no breach/deadline),
but a notable structural development for the broader market, relevant to
how CLM customers think about CA diversification.
