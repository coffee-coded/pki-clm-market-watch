## Watch item (not yet significant)

### The "October 1" 200-day certificate expiration wave — still no confirmed major outage
Certificates issued right after the CA/Browser Forum's March 15, 2026
cutover to 200-day max validity hit their expiry window Oct 1. Mainstream
press (TechRadar) ran "could break the internet" coverage, citing research
that ~40% of enterprises aren't ready. As of this digest, still no named,
confirmed major outage — just continued risk/analysis coverage repeating
the same warning. Keep watching: if a concrete outage lands, that's a
SIGNIFICANT follow-up.
