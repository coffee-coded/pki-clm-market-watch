## Significant

### Certificate-management bug behind multi-day, worldwide Microsoft 365 outage
A global Microsoft 365 outage (Aug 31–Sep 3) knocked out Exchange Online,
Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Purview, Defender XDR, Copilot and
Universal Print for ~48 hours. Microsoft's own post-incident account
attributed it to a code update to its internal certificate-management system
that silently stopped distributing new authentication certificates until the
old ones expired. Hyperscaler-scale proof of the "expired/mismanaged
certificate causes outages" thesis this whole market sells against.
[Findings detail](../findings/2026-09-15.md)

### FIPS 140-2 sunset: deadline arrived, with a federal-enforcement follow-on
Flagged 2026-09-15 as an approaching deadline; landed today (2026-09-21).
NIST's CMVP has moved all remaining FIPS 140-2 certificates to Historical
status — new federal procurements can no longer cite them, though existing
deployments keep running. Follow-up detail from today: for DoD contractors
under CMMC/DFARS, the practical enforcement bite follows on **November 10,
2026**, when CMMC Level 2 assessments start checking for this. Worth flagging
forward to any watchlist vendor selling FIPS-validated crypto into
defense/federal accounts.

- **DigiCert research on certificate-failure costs** (Sep 9) — vendor-sponsored
  survey: 34% of orgs hit an outage from an expired cert, 40% report downtime
  from cert mismanagement, ~1 in 4 report a worst-incident cost over $250K.
- **PQC key-discovery survey** (Sep 10) — industry research finding
  organizations discover far more keys/certs than expected once they start a
  PQC migration; frames 2026 as the year PQC deadlines "moved up" (citing
  June's Executive Order 14412 and Microsoft's own accelerated timeline).
- **CA/Browser Forum Ballot SMC017v2** — S/MIME CA Root/Sub-CA minimum RSA key
  size rose from 2048 to 4096 bits for keys created after Sep 15, 2026 (that
  threshold has now passed). Narrower than the TLS 47-day ballot but another
  crypto-agility deadline for CAs and any vendor managing S/MIME certs.
