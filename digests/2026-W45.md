# Weekly Digest — 2026-W45 (Oct 27–Nov 2, 2026)

Covers `findings/2026-10-27.md` and `findings/2026-10-31.md`. Oct 28–30 and
Nov 1 were quiet. Three SIGNIFICANT items this week — a CA-trust-chain
incident escalating to a market exit, a second major CA incident at a
different vendor, and a correction to this tracker's own terminology for
the Venafi/CyberArk product line.

## Significant

### eMudhra exits the TLS certificate business; Chrome's root removal lands
Following the Oct 17–20 disclosure that eight eMudhra intermediate CA
private keys were trivially recoverable (logged 2026-10-26), eMudhra
announced Oct 22 it is exiting the TLS certificate business entirely, and
Chrome's removal of its emSign ECC Root CA - G3 rolled out Oct 27,
breaking any site that hadn't migrated to another trusted chain. A
publicly trusted CA voluntarily exiting the TLS market after a
key-compromise incident is a rare, market-moving event.
[Findings detail](../findings/2026-10-27.md)

### GoDaddy mass-revokes ~400,000 TLS certificates over a CP/CPS compliance bug
GoDaddy (via its Starfield CA brand) revoked roughly 400,000 TLS
certificates after a third party found a mismatch between the
certificates' policy OIDs/CPS URI qualifiers and Starfield's published
CP/CPS — a documentation/compliance failure, not a key compromise. This is
the third major CA-ecosystem incident logged by this tracker in about six
weeks (ccTLD DNS-hijack, eMudhra key compromise, now this), each a
different failure mode. Keyfactor's CSO Chris Hickman commented publicly,
tying it to the watchlist's crypto-agility narrative.
[Findings detail](../findings/2026-10-31.md)

### Correction: CyberArk Certificate Manager was rebranded "Next-Generation Trust Security (NGTS)" under Palo Alto Networks back in March 2026
This tracker's own 2026-10-08 correction ("CyberArk Certificate Manager" as
the authoritative name) is now itself outdated. Palo Alto Networks
announced NGTS on March 23, 2026 — predating this tracker's Sept 15 start
date — folding the SaaS certificate-manager product into Strata Cloud
Manager. CyberArk Certificate Manager's own pages now redirect to NGTS; a
self-hosted product continues as "CyberArk Trust Protection Foundation."
This tracker will now refer to the SaaS product as **"Palo Alto Networks
Next-Generation Trust Security (NGTS)"** going forward — the third naming
correction for this product line in six weeks (Idira → CyberArk
Certificate Manager → NGTS). `watchlist.md`'s current primary-vendor entry
("Venafi (now part of CyberArk)") is now two rebrands behind; worth a
manual update by whoever maintains it.

## Process note

Three major CA-ecosystem incidents in six weeks (ccTLD hijack, eMudhra,
GoDaddy) is a notable acceleration compared to the tracker's first month,
when such incidents were rare and often caught only via backfill. All
three surfaced within days of disclosure this time, suggesting the
dedicated CA-incident sweep query is working as intended — though it may
also just reflect an unusually active stretch for CA operational failures
industry-wide.
