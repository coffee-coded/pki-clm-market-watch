### Idira (formerly CyberArk) rolling out new Machine Identity Management Marketplace, phased Sept 30 onward
- **Date:** License-file changes effective September 6, 2026; Marketplace Phase 1 launches September 30, 2026
- **Source:** CyberArk community, https://community.cyberark.com/s/article/CyberArk-is-now-Idira-Frequently-Asked-Questions ; Palo Alto Networks docs, https://docs.cyberark.com/whats-new/latest/en/
- **Summary:** As part of the ongoing CyberArk→Idira transition (flagged 2026-09-25), Certificate Manager/PKI customers are being migrated to a new Machine Identity Management Marketplace on the Palo Alto Networks Developer Portal, launching in phases starting September 30, 2026. License-file downloads already moved off the old CyberArk Community as of September 6. Operational/administrative rather than a new capability, but worth noting for any Idira/Venafi Certificate Manager customer who needs to re-locate license files or integrations during the transition.
