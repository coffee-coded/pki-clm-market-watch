### [SIGNIFICANT] CyberArk (Venafi's parent) rebranded as "Idira" by Palo Alto Networks — missed until now
- **Date:** Rebrand announced/rolled out starting May 31, 2026 (Phase 1); surfaced in this tracker today, 2026-09-25
- **Source:** CyberArk community FAQ, https://community.cyberark.com/s/article/CyberArk-is-now-Idira-Frequently-Asked-Questions ; SDxCentral, https://www.sdxcentral.com/news/palo-alto-networks-rebrands-cyberark-with-idira-platform-release/ ; Palo Alto Networks SEC Form 8-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1327567/000119312526045600/d40626dex991.htm
- **Summary:** Following its ~$25B acquisition of CyberArk (completed Feb 11, 2026), Palo Alto Networks folded the entire CyberArk portfolio into a single new brand, **Idira**, positioned as a "next-generation identity security platform for the AI enterprise" extending PAM to machine identities and AI agents. Phase 1 (UI/logo/docs) began May 31, 2026, rolling out in phases since. **Practical effect for this watchlist: "CyberArk Certificate Manager" (formerly Venafi TLS Protect) is now sold as "Idira Certificate Manager."** This tracker's baseline digest (2026-09-15) covered the Palo Alto/CyberArk acquisition but missed this rebrand entirely, and every daily search since has kept returning "CyberArk Certificate Manager" / "Next-Gen Trust Security" without surfacing the Idira name until today. **Recommend updating `watchlist.md`'s "Venafi (now part of CyberArk)" line to reference Idira going forward**, and searching "Idira Certificate Manager" / "Idira" alongside "Venafi"/"CyberArk" in future runs so this vendor doesn't keep slipping through.
