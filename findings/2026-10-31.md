### [SIGNIFICANT] GoDaddy mass-revokes ~400,000+ TLS certificates over a CP/CPS compliance bug
- **Date:** Revocation Oct 21, 2026; GoDaddy update Oct 23, 2026; Keyfactor CSO commentary Oct 23, 2026
- **Source:** Hackread, https://hackread.com/godaddy-revokes-ssl-certificates-policy-errors/ ; Unwire.hk, https://unwire.hk/2026/10/23/godaddy-ssl-certificates-revoked-compliance-2026/tech-secure/ ; Safeguard, https://safeguard.sh/resources/blog/godaddy-mass-revocation-403643-tls-certificates
- **Summary:** GoDaddy (via its Starfield CA brand) revoked roughly 400,000 TLS certificates after a third party found the certificates' policy object identifiers (OIDs) and CPS URI qualifiers didn't match Starfield's published CP/CPS — a compliance/documentation mismatch, not a private-key compromise. Sources differ slightly on the exact count (404,607 vs. a corrected 403,643, of which ~378,067 were actually revoked and the rest had already expired). Site owners had to re-issue and reinstall certificates quickly to avoid browser warnings or outages. This is the third major CA-ecosystem incident this tracker has logged in about six weeks (ccTLD DNS-hijack, eMudhra key compromise, now this) — each a different failure mode (DNS validation, key generation, CP/CPS compliance), reinforcing the same theme of a fragile, operationally brittle public-CA trust model. Keyfactor's CSO Chris Hickman commented publicly on the incident (per Hackread), directly tying it to the watchlist's crypto-agility/automated-CLM narrative.

### [SIGNIFICANT] Correction/backfill: CyberArk Certificate Manager was rebranded "Next-Generation Trust Security (NGTS)" under Palo Alto Networks back in March 2026 — missed until now
- **Date:** Palo Alto Networks press release March 23, 2026; discovered by this tracker 2026-10-31
- **Source:** Palo Alto Networks, https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/network-security/next-gen-trust-security/certificate-manager ; investor press release, https://investors.paloaltonetworks.com/news-releases/news-release-details/palo-alto-networks-introduces-next-generation-trust-security ; Palo Alto docs, https://docs.paloaltonetworks.com/next-gen-trust-security
- **Summary:** This tracker previously corrected itself on 2026-10-08, concluding "CyberArk Certificate Manager" (not "Idira Certificate Manager") was the authoritative current name for the Venafi-descended product line. That correction is now itself outdated: Palo Alto Networks announced "Next-Generation Trust Security" (NGTS) back on March 23, 2026 — predating this tracker's own Sept 15, 2026 start date — folding certificate lifecycle management, PKI, and certificate automation into a SaaS offering integrated with Strata Cloud Manager, explicitly positioned around automating certificate renewal ahead of the 47-day mandate and post-quantum readiness. CyberArk Certificate Manager's own web pages now redirect to NGTS; a self-hosted product continues separately under the "CyberArk Trust Protection Foundation" name. **Going forward, this tracker will refer to the SaaS product as "Palo Alto Networks Next-Generation Trust Security (NGTS)"** rather than "CyberArk Certificate Manager," while continuing to note its Venafi/CyberArk lineage. This is now the third naming correction this tracker has had to make for this product line in six weeks (Idira → CyberArk Certificate Manager → NGTS), underscoring how fast-moving and confusing the post-acquisition branding has been — worth flagging explicitly to whoever maintains `watchlist.md`, since its current primary-vendor line ("Venafi (now part of CyberArk)") is now two rebrands behind.
