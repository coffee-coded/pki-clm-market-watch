### [SIGNIFICANT] Certificate-management bug behind multi-day, worldwide Microsoft 365 outage
- **Date:** August 31 – September 3, 2026
- **Source:** TechCrunch, https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/microsoft-tests-fix-for-latest-hours-long-outlook-outage/ ; BleepingComputer, https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/microsoft-exchange-online-outage-causes-email-failures-auth-issues/ ; Born's Tech and Windows World, https://borncity.com/win/2026/08/31/ex1464935-m365-outage-due-to-forgotten-certificate-renewal-august-31-2026/
- **Summary:** A global Microsoft 365 outage starting August 31 knocked out Exchange Online, Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Purview, Defender XDR, Copilot and Universal Print for roughly 48 hours, with some services still degraded days later. Early reports pointed to an expired authentication certificate; Microsoft's own post-incident account attributed it to a code update to its internal certificate-management system that silently stopped distributing a subset of new authentication certificates, which only surfaced once the old ones expired. Directly validates the "expired/mismanaged certificate as a recurring outage cause" thesis this watchlist tracks, at hyperscaler scale — strong talking point for Keyfactor/AppViewX/Venafi sales and marketing.

### [SIGNIFICANT] FIPS 140-2 certificates move to "Historical" status September 21, 2026 — hard federal-procurement deadline
- **Date:** Deadline is September 21, 2026 (ongoing coverage through September 2026)
- **Source:** SafeLogic, https://www.safelogic.com/blog/what-happens-on-september-21-2026 ; NIST CSRC, https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/fips-140-3-transition-effort ; Keyfactor newsroom (context), https://www.keyfactor.com/newsroom/
- **Summary:** On September 21, 2026 (six days from this report), NIST's CMVP moves all remaining FIPS 140-2 module certificates to "Historical" status. Historical-list modules can't be cited to satisfy new federal FIPS-validation requirements, so any vendor or CLM buyer relying solely on FIPS 140-2 validation loses eligibility for new federal contracts unless they've migrated to FIPS 140-3. Existing deployments keep running, but this is a hard near-term deadline CLM/PKI buyers selling into government need to act on now; Keyfactor is actively publishing guidance tied to it.
