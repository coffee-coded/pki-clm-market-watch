### Cloudflare announces plans to become a public Certificate Authority
- **Date:** Announced September 29, 2026; surfaced in this tracker today, 2026-11-16
- **Source:** Dark Reading, https://www.darkreading.com/cloud-security/cloudflare-announces-public-certificate-authority-post-quantum-web ; ABI Research, https://www.abiresearch.com/market-research/insight/7788625-cloudflare-to-serve-as-a-certificate-autho ; IT Brief UK, https://itbrief.co.uk/story/cloudflare-to-launch-public-certificate-authority-service
- **Summary:** Cloudflare announced plans to operate its own public CA, issuing both classical TLS certificates and post-quantum Merkle Tree Certificates (MTCs, free) via ACME, backed initially by a GlobalSign root with applications pending in four browser root programs. Post-quantum issuance is targeted for early 2027; as of late September Cloudflare had not yet issued certificates itself, having previously sourced them from 16 partner CAs. This follows Cloudflare's 2026 MTC experiment with Google, which it began winding down in August. Analyst reaction is mixed: a second free, high-volume issuer (alongside Let's Encrypt, which issues over half of public certs) is seen by some as healthy for the ecosystem's resilience, while others flag the risk of concentrating both CDN and CA roles in one provider. Doesn't cleanly meet this tracker's SIGNIFICANT criteria (no watchlist vendor, no breach/outage, no hard regulatory deadline), but it's a notable structural development for the broader CLM/PKI market this watchlist tracks — a large new entrant to the public-CA space is relevant context for how Keyfactor/AppViewX/CyberArk Certificate Manager (NGTS) customers think about CA diversification and crypto-agility.
