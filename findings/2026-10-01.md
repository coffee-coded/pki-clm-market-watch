### Mainstream coverage warns October 1, 2026 could trigger a wave of SSL/TLS outages as first 200-day certificates expire
- **Date:** October 1, 2026
- **Source:** TechRadar, https://www.techradar.com/pro/why-october-1-2026-could-be-the-day-ssl-tls-certificates-break-the-internet ; SSLCalendar, https://sslcalendar.com/blog/october-2026-ssl-certificate-expiration-wave/
- **Summary:** Certificates issued right after the CA/Browser Forum's March 15, 2026 cutover to 200-day maximum validity are now hitting their expiry window, and multiple outlets (including mainstream tech press, not just vendor blogs) are warning this could cause widespread outages, with cited research claiming ~40% of enterprises aren't ready. No specific major outage has been confirmed as of this report — this is a risk/analysis story, not an incident report. Directly validates this watchlist's core thesis (shrinking cert lifespans drive outages drive CLM demand) and is worth checking the next few days for whether a concrete, named outage materializes, which would be a SIGNIFICANT follow-up.

### DigiCert sunsets discovery/managed automation features in legacy CertCentral platform
- **Date:** End-of-life effective October 1, 2026
- **Source:** DigiCert Trust Calendar / announcements, https://www.digicert.com/trust-calendar ; https://knowledge.digicert.com/announcements
- **Summary:** DigiCert ended support for certificate discovery and managed automation within its legacy CertCentral platform as of today, pushing customers toward its newer Trust Lifecycle Manager product. Routine platform lifecycle/product-sunset news, relevant to any CertCentral customers relying on those specific features.
