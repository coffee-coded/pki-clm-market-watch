### CA/Browser Forum Ballot SMC017v2 minimum-RSA-key-size requirement takes effect
- **Date:** Ballot finalized July 30, 2026; threshold effective September 15, 2026
- **Source:** CA/Browser Forum, https://cabforum.org/2026/06/16/ballot-smc-017v2/
- **Summary:** Under Ballot SMC017v2 (S/MIME Baseline Requirements v1.0.15), CA Root and Subordinate certificates must use a minimum 4096-bit RSA key (up from 2048-bit) for any key created after September 15, 2026 — that threshold landed yesterday. Subscriber certificates keep the 2048-bit floor for now, but by September 15, 2027 CAs must stop issuing subscriber certs from any Sub-CA with a key smaller than 3072 bits, phasing out legacy 2048-bit Sub-CAs. Narrower in scope than the TLS 47-day ballot (S/MIME/email certs, not general TLS), but another crypto-agility deadline CA-side infrastructure and any watchlist vendor issuing/managing S/MIME certs need to track.
