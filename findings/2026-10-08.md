### [SIGNIFICANT] AppViewX ships Agent Kill Switch and Shadow AI discovery for Agent Identity Security
- **Date:** October 6, 2026
- **Source:** GlobeNewswire, https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/06/3375617/0/en/appviewx-adds-shadow-ai-visibility-and-a-runtime-kill-switch-to-agent-identity-security.html ; Help Net Security, https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/06/appviewx-shadow-ai-visibility/
- **Summary:** AppViewX shipped a major expansion of its Agent Identity Security product (built on the Eos acquisition): an "Agent Kill Switch" that can stop AI agents and their sessions automatically (event-driven), via runtime policy, or manually — covering both sanctioned and unauthorized ("shadow") agents without requiring an MCP gateway — plus shadow-AI discovery (unsanctioned/browser-based/short-lived script agents), a new MCP Gateway for discovering and risk-scoring MCP servers, agent identities chained to AppViewX-managed PKI, and AI token-usage/cost controls. This is a primary-vendor major product capability (meets this watchlist's SIGNIFICANT bar directly) and lands three weeks after DigiCert's competing AI Trust Manager kill switch (GA Sept 15) — confirms the AI-agent-identity space is now an active, fast-moving three-way (at least) competitive front among AppViewX, DigiCert, and Keyfactor (via its pending Cofide acquisition). Note: coverage is largely vendor press release language repeated by trade outlets, not independent review — treat capability claims ("industry's most comprehensive") as marketing.

### Correction to 2026-09-25 finding: Venafi/Certificate Manager's Idira status is more nuanced than reported
- **Date:** Correction made today, 2026-10-08
- **Source:** CyberArk community FAQ, https://community.cyberark.com/s/article/CyberArk-is-now-Idira-Frequently-Asked-Questions
- **Summary:** The 2026-09-25 finding in this tracker stated flatly that "Venafi is now sold as Idira Certificate Manager." Re-checking the primary source today: CyberArk's own official FAQ states the **Certificates and PKI portfolio (formerly Venafi) is explicitly the exception to the Idira rename** and remains CyberArk-branded, at least through this first rollout phase — while various third-party sites (resellers, comparison blogs) do call it "Idira Certificate Manager," which appears to be premature or imprecise. **Net effect on this watchlist: continue tracking this product under "CyberArk Certificate Manager" as the authoritative current name, not "Idira Certificate Manager," until CyberArk's own documentation says otherwise** — the broader Idira rebrand itself (as a corporate/parent-brand event) remains accurate and significant, but the specific claim about the Certificate Manager product name was overstated.
