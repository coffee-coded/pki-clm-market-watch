### DigiCert launched "AI Trust Manager" with an agent kill switch — actual GA date was Sept 15, missed until now
- **Date:** General availability announced September 15, 2026 (same day as this tracker's baseline run); surfaced today, 2026-10-04
- **Source:** GlobeNewswire, https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/15/3362163/0/en/every-ai-agent-needs-a-kill-switch-digicert-launches-ai-trust-manager.html ; DigiCert, https://www.digicert.com/news/every-ai-agent-needs-a-kill-switch-digicert-launches-ai-trust-manager
- **Summary:** DigiCert launched AI Trust Manager (part of DigiCert ONE), which issues a cryptographically signed "AI Passport" to verify an AI agent's identity and ownership, attaches policy-based permissions ("visas") limiting what systems/data/actions it can access, and includes an automated kill switch that can revoke an agent's authority and quarantine it if it attempts a prohibited action. Directly competitive with Keyfactor's Cofide acquisition and AppViewX's Eos acquisition, both aimed at the same AI-agent-identity space — all three watchlist-adjacent vendors are now converging on "verified, revocable AI agent identity" as a product category. Not SIGNIFICANT under this watchlist's criteria (DigiCert is a secondary vendor, so the "major product launch" bar applies only to Keyfactor/AppViewX/Venafi), but important competitive context, and another reminder that routine PR-style searches can miss same-day launches from secondary vendors when attention is on the primary three.
